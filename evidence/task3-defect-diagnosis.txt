Task 3 - Booking Capacity Defect Diagnosis

File: src/main/java/edu/university/equipment/BookingService.java
Defective condition: quantity > e.getAvailableQuantity() + 1
Root cause: Adding 1 to available inventory permits a request for one extra unit.
Correction: Compare requested quantity directly with available inventory.
Required rule: quantity > 0 and quantity <= available inventory.
Safety requirement: Reject invalid requests before modifying inventory or creating bookings.
